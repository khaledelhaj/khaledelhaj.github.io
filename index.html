NPI Fast-Track Scheduling

New products start as 6–12 wafer setup lots. The faster they reach the customer, the better the odds of winning the business. Hundreds ran each year.

  • Wrote the spec for a scheduler that predicts when a setup lot will arrive and holds the next tool open, so it's ready when the lot gets there
  • Made the trade-off: accept 2–4 hours of idle time on a tool like a batch diffusion furnace rather than make the NPI lot wait 4 hours
  • Ended the manual workaround of pulling tools out of full auto, which often cost hours of production when nobody put them back
  • Rolled it out with process, production and equipment engineers, and trained the floor on why their tool was waiting
  • Full Auto Rate on setup lots rose from 30% to 95%, with a record 0.4 days per masking layer. Three of these products grew to about 40% of fab capacity.
